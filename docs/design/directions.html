What all three share

Material 3 Expressive underneath Compose's components, accessibility and motion. A direction changes only colour, type, shape and motion.
Light and dark Follows the phone's setting, with an override in Settings.
Dynamic colour as an option Turn it on and your wallpaper's colours replace the palette. Type, shapes and motion stay.
Drawers Each place your files live, the phone, the NAS or an SFTP server, is a drawer at the top of Browse.
Honest progress A transfer says what it's doing, how far it got and how long is left, and it can always be paused.
Readable first Text at 4.5:1 or better in both modes, 48 dp touch targets, and file types told apart by icon shape before colour.
